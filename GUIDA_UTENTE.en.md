# User Guide

Welcome to **CommanderOne**, the social tracker for Magic: The Gathering Commander games, built for your playgroup.

## Your group

CommanderOne is organized around independent **playgroups**: create your own group or join an existing one with an invite code. Each group has members, decks, games, events and stats that are completely separate from every other group.

- **Create a group**: you automatically become its administrator and get an invite code to share with friends.
- **Join with a code**: ask a member of the group you want to reach for the invite code.
- If you're part of multiple groups, a switcher at the top lets you jump between them.

## Feed

The home screen shows a summary of the current season, the mini leaderboard, the deck of the moment, this week's activity and your group's latest games/notifications.

## Play

This is where you log a new game: pick the players at the table, their decks, the winner and — optionally — the elimination order and who eliminated whom.

## Events

Your group's calendar: RSVP to upcoming events, and if you're an administrator you can run tournaments (multiplayer pods or 1v1 pairings with Swiss standings).

## Group

Your group's stats: season leaderboard, players, decks and game history.

## Decks

Manage your decks: name, commander, colors, power level (bracket), archetype and card list. You can import a decklist from Archidekt or paste one manually.

## Achievements

26 unlockable milestones as you play: some public, others secret until unlocked. They're tracked separately for each group you're part of.

## Judge Bot

Got a rules question? Ask the Judge Bot in its dedicated section: it answers with a ruling, an explanation and the official rules it cites.
