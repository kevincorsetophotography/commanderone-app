# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 19, 2026*

> **Note for whoever runs CommanderOne**: this document is a technically accurate draft — it describes exactly what the code does, who it shares data with and why — but **it does not replace a legal review**. The field in `[square brackets]` about the chosen hosting provider (section 5.5) still needs completing once a provider is chosen; before publishing it, have the text checked by someone familiar with GDPR/privacy compliance in your jurisdiction regardless, especially if you expect underage or non-EU users.

## 1. Data controller

CommanderOne is developed and operated by **Kevin Corseto**, reachable at **ckappa97@gmail.com**.

This notice applies to the CommanderOne web app (the "Service") and describes what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it.

## 2. What CommanderOne is

CommanderOne is a social tracker for Magic: The Gathering Commander games, organized around independent play groups ("Groups"). It is not an official Wizards of the Coast product — see section 9.

## 3. Data we collect

### 3.1 Data you give us directly

| Data | When | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Username | Registration | Yes |
| Email | Registration | Yes — used for account verification and password recovery |
| Password | Registration | Yes — we never see it in plain text: it's hashed (bcrypt) before being stored |
| Avatar | Optional, any time | No — you pick an existing Magic card as your profile picture; you don't upload photos of yourself |
| Group name, decks, games, comments, reactions, event RSVPs | Normal app usage | Only if you choose to use that feature |
| Judge Bot questions | Optional usage | No |

### 3.2 Data generated by using the Service

Game results, stats, unlocked achievements and notifications are generated automatically based on how you and your Group use the Service.

### 3.3 Technical data

IP address (used only to rate-limit login attempts/abuse — not stored permanently linked to your profile), creation/modification timestamps of content.

### 3.4 What we do NOT collect

We don't collect payment data (the Service has no paid features), we don't track your geographic location, and we don't use advertising-profiling cookies.

## 4. How we use your data

- **Providing the Service**: creating your account, granting you access to the Groups you're part of, showing you your Group's decks/games/stats/events.
- **Essential communications**: address-verification and password-reset emails. We don't send marketing emails.
- **Security**: limiting repeated access attempts, detecting abuse.
- **Judge Bot**: if you ask the Judge Bot a question, its text is processed to give you an answer (see section 5.3).

Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): performance of the contract for using the Service for the points above; legitimate interest for anti-abuse security.

## 5. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data to anyone. We share it only with the technical providers necessary to run the Service ("data processors"), and with the other members of your Group to the extent a feature is inherently shared (e.g. your username is visible to your Group's members, not to strangers).

### 5.1 Other members of your Group

Username, avatar, decks, games, comments, reactions and Judge Bot questions you post are visible to other members of **your own Group** — that's the social nature of the Service. They are not visible to members of other Groups: isolation between Groups is a structural principle of the architecture (see also `CLAUDE.md` in the repository).

### 5.2 Resend (transactional email)

We use [Resend](https://resend.com) to send address-verification and password-reset emails. Resend receives your email address for the sole purpose of delivering these emails. See [Resend's privacy policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

### 5.3 Groq (Judge Bot)

If you use the Judge Bot, your question (and, where relevant, card names and excerpts from official rules) is sent to [Groq](https://groq.com) to generate an answer using AI language models. Please don't include personal data about yourself or others in your questions. See [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/).

If the Judge Bot has hit its daily usage cap or the AI is unavailable, your question is still answered using only official public data (card text and the rulebook) without involving Groq.

### 5.4 Scryfall (Magic card data and images)

To show card images and information (including avatars) we query the public [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) API. These requests contain only card names/IDs, never your personal data. See the [Scryfall terms](https://scryfall.com/docs/terms).

## 6. How long we keep data

We keep your data as long as your account stays active. If you delete your account (see section 7.3), your personal data (username, email, password) is deleted immediately. The exception is data that's part of the **shared history** of games played with other members of your Group (e.g. your participation in a game, the deck you used): to avoid altering the history of people who played with you and haven't asked to delete their own account, these references are anonymized (reassigned to a "deleted user" account with no identifying data) instead of being deleted along with the rest.

## 7. Your rights

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a jurisdiction with equivalent rights, you have the right to:

- **Access**: know what data we hold about you.
- **Rectification**: correct inaccurate data (e.g. from the Account page).
- **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")**: delete your account yourself at any time from Account → Danger zone, without having to contact anyone. See section 6 for what gets anonymized instead of deleted, and why.
- **Portability**: receive an export of your Group's data in a machine-readable format (JSON) — available to Group admins from the Admin section.
- **Objection and restriction**: object to processing based on legitimate interest, or ask for it to be restricted.
- **Complaint**: file a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali — [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

To exercise a right that isn't available to you directly in the app, write to **ckappa97@gmail.com**.

### 7.1 If you're the only administrator of a Group

Account deletion is blocked if you're the only administrator of a Group with other members, until you promote someone else to admin — otherwise the other members would lose governance of the Group without anyone having explicitly granted it to them. If you're the only member of the Group, however, the entire Group is deleted along with your account.

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt) and are never readable in plain text, not even by whoever administers the Service. Sessions use JWT tokens with an expiry. We limit the number of access attempts to prevent automated attacks. No system is 100% secure: if we discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

## 9. Relationship with Wizards of the Coast

CommanderOne is unofficial "Fan Content", permitted under Wizards of the Coast's Fan Content Policy. It is not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. Wizards of the Coast does not receive or process the personal data collected by this Service.

## 10. Minimum age

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you're between 13 and 16 (or the age of digital consent set by your country, if different), make sure you have permission from whoever holds parental responsibility before creating an account.

## 11. Local storage (we don't use tracking cookies)

The Service stores your access token and active Group in your browser's *local storage* — a technical mechanism needed to keep you signed in, not an advertising or third-party tracking cookie. Clearing it (e.g. by clearing the site's data in your browser) automatically logs you out.

## 12. Changes to this notice

If we make a substantial update to this notice, we'll flag it in the Service before the changes take effect.
